The big idea

Tidewater turns official tide tables into a plain answer for paddlers: when to launch and when to be back. Tidewater is live, and the club's other tools take their tide times from it.

What it unlocks

  1. Only safe trips get bookedHarbor Log blocks unsafe tide windows.
  2. A baseline for the sensorsBuoy Watch spots a broken sensor.
  3. Tides without a signalCoastline Maps prints them offline.
