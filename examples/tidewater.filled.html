Why it matters

Paddlers plan every trip around the tide, and a wrong time can strand a group on a mudflat or put them in a fast current. Tidewater turns the official tables into a plain answer, and it is the one place the club's other tools get that answer from.

What it unlocks for your other projects

  • Harbor Log. It blocks bookings for unsafe tide windows, so the booking site never sells a trip that cannot run.
  • Buoy Watch. Its predictions are the baseline the volunteers' sensors are compared against, which is how a broken sensor gets spotted.
  • Coastline Maps. The offline map packs print the next week of tides from it, so paddlers without a signal still have them.

Glossary

Tide tables
The official yearly list of predicted high and low water for each station.
Station
A named point on the coast with its own tide predictions.
Low water
The lowest point of the tide, when boats launching can run aground.
